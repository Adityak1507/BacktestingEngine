# ML direction classifier: results

Generated by `python examples/ml_direction.py`. Every number below is out of sample.

## Setup

- **Data**: daily bars for ORCL (1995–2014), YHOO (1996–2015), NVDA (1999–2014), GOOG (2004–2013), 16,161 bars in total, adjusted for dividends.
- **Label**: 1 if the stock rises from the next open to the following open (the return a signal at today's close can actually capture).
- **Features**: 19 technical indicators known at today's close (returns, volatility, RSI, distance from moving averages, Bollinger z-score, range, gap, volume z-score).
- **Walk-forward**: retrain every 63 bars on known labels only (purge gap of 2 bars); 3 years minimum history.
- **Selection**: models and decision rules were compared on predictions **before 2010** only. Predictions **from 2010** are a holdout scored once.
